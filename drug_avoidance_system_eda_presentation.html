Key EDA Results

Condition & Term Highlights
  • Hypertension has the most distinct listed drugs (126), followed by Acne (115) and Pain (99).
  • Most frequent side-effect terms: lips (1,210), tongue (1,188), and hives (1,087). Term frequency alone does not equal clinical danger.
  • Guselkumab and ubrogepant have the fewest extracted side-effect terms (1 each).
Warning Descriptions & Clinical Caution
  • Selected drugs show warning terms in 100% of reviewed descriptions.
  • This measures textual documentation, not patient occurrence rate.
  • Physicians must evaluate warning terms when prescribing medication.
Distribution & Outlier Findings (IQR Method)

Ratings are left-skewed (-0.81) spanning 0 to 10 with median ~7. Review counts are heavily right-skewed (6.11) with extreme outliers reaching nearly 3,000 reviews.

Insight: As seen from the plot, the outliers are true values since ratings and reviews can differ between each drug and each person. That is why some values reach 3,000 reviews or a rating of 2. From observing the plot, we can also tell that there are no invalid values such as negative numbers.

Comprehensive Insights & Key Findings

Rating Distribution

Ratings for drugs associated with top conditions are mostly distributed between 6 and 9. The plot does not show that side effects directly suppressed user scores.

Reviews & Correlation

Most records have few reviews, while a small number reach ~3,000. Pearson correlation between rating and reviews is -0.043 (very weak negative relationship).

Skewness Dynamics

Ratings are left-skewed (-0.81), indicating generally high satisfaction. Review counts are heavily right-skewed (6.11 / 6.14).

Retention of Outliers

Outliers were retained as valid domain values (popular drugs gather thousands of reviews; rating of 2 is real). No invalid negative values were detected.

Class & Prescription Imbalance

Rx is the dominant prescription group (1,265 out of 1,562). The target label avoid_drug is imbalanced (915 flagged vs 647 not flagged), reflecting real-world clinical distributions.

Conclusion & Recommendations

1

Pattern Identification

The EDA successfully identifies clear structured patterns in side-effect text frequency, condition-drug availability, user ratings, review volume, and prescription class balances.

2

Frequency vs. Clinical Risk

Frequently extracted side-effect terms (lips, tongue, hives) and high user ratings do not by themselves establish clinical safety or occurrence rate without professional medical review.

3

Non-Clinical Disclaimer

Doctors must evaluate explicit warning text when prescribing medication. The project-defined avoid_drug label serves as an analytical benchmark, not a clinical prescription recommendation.
